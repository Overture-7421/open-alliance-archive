## <span style="color:#6b35aa">Practice Field Construction Update</span>

Over the past few days, we’ve made progress on building our half field, which will be crucial for testing our robot and practicing our autonomous routines.

Thanks to one of our sponsors, Equitek, for helping us cut the wood for the field.

<hr>

## <span style="color:#6b35aa">Half Field Assembly Status</span>

At this point, most of our half field is assembled and ready to use. The primary structures are in place, and we’ve already begun using the field for early testing.

Remaining items:

- Polycarbonate panels for the top of the hub

- Final assembly of the tower

- Minor alignment and cleanup

We are also setting up the AprilTags across the entire half field. This will allow us to test our vision system and autonomous routines in a realistic environment as soon as possible.

## <span style="color:#6b35aa">Swerve Drive Ramp Testing</span>

With much of the field assembled, we began testing the ramps using our swerve drive to evaluate whether the robot could get stuck, high-center, or lose traction while transitioning on and off the ramp.

These tests help us:

- Identify potential clearance issues

- Check if we could get stuck on the ramp.
