## <span style="color:#6b35aa">Turret Simulation Testing</span>

At the same time, the **software team** has been hard at work getting the turret fully functional in simulation.

Some of the things we have been testing include:

- **Position-based aiming**
- **Compensating turret motion based on chassis rotational speed**
- **Shoot While Move**

These tests allow us to validate turret control behavior before full hardware integration and reduce iteration time once the system is fully assembled.
