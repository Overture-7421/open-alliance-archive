## <span style="color:#6b35aa">Electrical & CAN Bus Layout</span>

With most of the base plates assembled, the **electrical team** has started working on wiring the robot.

We’ve begun wiring:

- **Drive motors**
- **Spindexer motors**

Like previous seasons, we are preparing two **CAN buses**:

- **CANivore Bus** → Drive motors, Pigeon, and swerve encoders
- **RoboRIO Bus** → Remaining mechanisms

This separation helps keep the drivetrain network isolated and organized.
