## <span style="color:#6b35aa">Intake Prototype Update</span>

During friday and saturday, we made significant progress on our intake prototype. As stated in a previous post, the base of our intake was inspired by WCP's CC intake. After some iterations, we were able to create a prototype that met some of our design goals:

- Small footprint to fit within the robot's frame perimeter.
- Lightweight design to minimize the impact on the robot's overall weight.

There are some details we want to improve on the prototype, such as adding polycarbonate rollers with silicone to improve impact absorption and grip on the game pieces.

## <span style="color:#6b35aa">Preliminary Robot Design</span>

The main robot design has been progressing well. We have completed a preliminary CAD model of the robot, which includes the new swerve modules, turret/shooter, spindexer, and intake. This design is not final and need a lot of improvements, but it gives us a good starting point. Right now we are focusing on dimensions and placements of the main subsystems to ensure everything fits within the robot's frame perimeterm, without compromising functionality.
