## <span style="color:#6b35aa">Monterrey Regional Recap</span>

Over the past week we have been hard at work preparing for our next event, but we wanted to take a moment to share our experience and results from the **Monterrey Regional**.

We finished qualifications ranked **12th** with a **5–5–0 record**, and went **3–2–0 in playoffs**, for a combined record of **8–7–0**.

We were selected as the **first pick of the 2nd alliance**, alongside:

- **6017 – Cyberius**
- **5932 – Quetzales**

Together, we made it all the way to the **finals**, where we fell to the 1st alliance.

We want to thank our alliance partners — we could not have made it that far without them.

While it was not the result we were aiming for, we are proud of our performance and the improvements we made throughout the event. We encountered several issues, but we were able to resolve most of them and learned a lot from the experience.

Although we did not qualify for Champs at the event, we were later selected as one of the **15 teams from the regional pool**, and we are excited to be heading to **Champs**.

## <span style="color:#6b35aa">Fails & Improvements</span>

Due to the chaos of the event, we were not able to capture many photos or videos, but here are some of the key issues we faced and how we addressed them:

- **Intake pulley failure**
  One of the pulleys driving the intake rollers was destroyed by the belt during a match.
  → **Fix:** Replaced it with a pulley with larger walls to better retain the belt

- **Shooter plate failure**
  A plate supporting the shooter motors bent and broke due to insufficient support. This had also happened prior to the event.
  → **Fix:** Added a support piece to reinforce the motor mounting area (epoxy alone was not sufficient)

- **Running on one shooter motor**
  At some point, the insert connecting one motor to the pulley fell out, leaving the motor spinning freely.
  → **Fix:** Reinstalled the insert

- **Odometry issues**
  One of our **Orange Pi** units failed, so we replaced it with a **Raspberry Pi**. The Raspberry Pi struggled to run our odometry pipeline and camera processing reliably.
  → **Fix:** Moved one working camera to the turret so it always had a clear view of the hub
  → This worked well until the camera cable failed in finals

- **Intake not closing fully (perimeter issue)**
  The intake no longer fully retracted, causing us to extend outside the frame perimeter and be bypassed in Playoffs Match 11. Likely caused by impacts and tight packaging.
  → **Fix:** Sanded down interference points to restore full closure
