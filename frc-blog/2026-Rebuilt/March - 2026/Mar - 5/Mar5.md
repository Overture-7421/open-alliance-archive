## <span style="color:#6b35aa">Robot Update</span>

Our **climber** has been assembled and integrated into the robot, and it seems to be working well so far (sadly no videos yet).

There are still a few small adjustments to make, but overall we are happy with how it turned out. We were able to program it very quickly thanks to **simulation** and the mechanism being a relatively simple **elevator-style climber**.

Some of the issues we are still working through include:

- Adding some type of **grip material** to prevent slipping on the bar
- The climber pushed us **over the weight limit** (more on that below)
- Increasing the climber height by about **one inch** to reduce the precision needed when grabbing the bar
- Fixing the **turret cable chain routing** to prevent interference with the climber

## <span style="color:#6b35aa">Shooter & Turret Tuning</span>

While the robot is now **mechanically complete**, we are still working through tuning and software integration.

Recently we have been tuning the **shooter** and **turret** so they can work together for our **shoot-on-the-move feature**. We are using a modified version of the system we developed in **2024**.

It is not fully competition-ready yet, but we expect it to be working well by the end of the weekend.

## <span style="color:#6b35aa">Weight Reduction</span>

Last weekend we weighed the robot and it came in at around **121 lbs**, which is over the **115 lb limit**.

During the week we managed to reduce the weight to around **116 lbs**, which is still slightly over the limit but moving in the right direction.

Some of the changes we made include:

- Removing one of the **SDS flywheels** (the shooter still appears to be consistent)
- Replacing some thicker frame components with **thinner structural parts**

There are still a few additional areas where we believe we can save weight, and we are hoping to reach **115 lbs by the end of the week**.
