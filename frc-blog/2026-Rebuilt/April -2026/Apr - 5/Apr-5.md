## <span style="color:#6b35aa">Intake Design</span>

For this version of Shelby, we went with a **rack and pinion intake**. One of the key design decisions was bringing back our **hybrid intake front**.

We used **2x1 aluminum tubing** split into three sections, connected using **polycarbonate plates**. This gives us a front structure that is:

- **Impact resistant**
- **Lightweight**
- Less prone to bending under collisions

## <span style="color:#6b35aa">Shooter Consistency Issues</span>

During testing, one of the main issues we faced was **fuel spread** after leaving the shooter.

We tested several approaches:

- Adjusting **shooter compression** → Made things worse
- Reducing **BPS** → Not enough improvement to justify performance loss
- Fixing a **compression inconsistency** → This helped significantly

After watching **Citrus Circuits’ Behind the Bumpers**, we noticed their discussion about changing the ball path from an **S-shape to a J-shape** into the shooter.

Fully implementing this would have required cutting new plates to improve compression consistency, which we did not have time for before Denver.

Instead, we implemented a faster solution by adding **3D printed components** to adjust the **ramp geometry** feeding the shooter.

<div align="center">

<em>Ramp geometry adjustment (blue = original, red = updated).</em>

</div>

This change improved shot consistency significantly and reduced spread.

While it does not completely eliminate the S-shape behavior, it is a solid improvement. We are planning to refine the full geometry further for **Championship**.
