## <span style="color:#6b35aa">Robot Design Updates - Krayon?? CAD</span>

So we have finally settled on a preliminary design for our robot, this time we did a Krayon (not so much Krayon) CAD. We got a little more detail to see how everything will fit together. Here are some of the main decisions we made:

- **Chassis Dimensions:** 26 inches wide by 28 inches long.
- **Swerve Modules:** Switching to SDS MK5N modules for better performance and reliability.
- **Intake System:** Intake on a pivot. After seeing WCPs CC intake, we decided to go with a similar design.
- **Elevator:** Elevator with a 2-stage design for climbing (Not final)
- **Turret/Shooter:** A turret with a flywheel shooter powered by two motors. With a hood for adjusting the angle for scoring/passing. We are going for a 1 inch compresion for consitency.
- **Spindexer:** After watching Mechanical Advantage and Team Rembrandts prototype their spindexers, we decided to implement a similar design for our robot, we are aiming to have a fast feeding/indexing system for our shooter.

This is not the final design, there are still many details to work out and changes to be made. But this is a good starting point for us to continue our design process. Some of the things are still in the works, such as:

- the elevator and climbing mechanism.
- turret and shooter design.
- Hopper walls and feeding mechanism.

## <span style="color:#6b35aa">Chassis</span>

As we said previously, we are changing our swerve modules to the new SDS MK5N modules. So, while most of the team is still working on prototyping and CAD. Software/Electrical teams have started building a temporaty chassis for testing out and tuning the new swerve modules. This chassis will have the 26x28 dimensions we are planning to use in competition.
