# <b><span style="color:#6b35aa">OffSeason's Recap</span></b>

</div>

Due to time constraints and we haven't been able to consistently post about our offseason activities, this post will serve as a brief overview of our 2025 FRC OffSeason results.

## <b><span style="color:#6b35aa">The Remix</span></b>

We competed in the The Remix for the third time in November and ended with a 4-5-0 record, placing 31st overall. We were the first pick of the 6th alliance and got eliminated on match 6 of the playoffs.

Some of the issues we had on this event were software related. Mostly we did were not able to finish coding our robot correctly and ended up writing the code as the event went on.

We only had one mechanical issue that caused us to not be able to grab algae. When building our grabber we mistakenly put the gears in the wrong order, which caused us to not have enough torque to hold the algae.

## <b><span style="color:#6b35aa">Mexico All-Star</span></b>

We also competed in the Mexico All-Star event this past october. We ended the event with a 6-2 record and placed 6th in the rankings. We were the first pick of the 2nd alliance and made it all the way to the finals for our third consecutive year in the event.
