Over the last couple of days we have been working on cutting and assembling the robot plates. We already cut most of our aluminum plates. After cutting the plates, we prepared them for painting by sanding them down.

(Two fotos, plates and assembly here)

After the pain was done we begun assembling the plates into our chassis. At the same time we started cutting our polycarbonate plates for the indexer and the intake.

(Polycarbonate cutting photo here)

3D printers are also hard at work printing mutiple parts for the robot.

After having most of the base plates assembled the electrical team has also started working on the wiring for the robot. We have been working on the wiring for the drive motors and the spindexer motors. Like previous season we are preparing two CAN Buses, CANivore and Roborio. The CANivore will be used for the drive motors, pigeon and swerve encoders. The roborio bus for the rest of the mechanisms.
